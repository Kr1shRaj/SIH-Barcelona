### Note on Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Visuals
The SCBA respirator, multi-gas detector, and safety harness visuals are rendered via dedicated composite 3D safety equipment geometry in A-Frame and Three.js (matching DGMS / Mines Act industrial safety standards) rather than repurposed third-party assets, ensuring trainees receive unambiguous and accurate equipment recognition cues.
